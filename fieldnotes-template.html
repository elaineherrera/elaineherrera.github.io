writing

occasional notes on places, processes, and paying attention.

2026.10 a practice of noticing

There is a particular kind of attention that only arrives when you walk without a destination. A change in the pavement. The same window, open for the first time. A tree you have passed a hundred times and never really seen.

I am trying to make more room for that kind of attention: to keep a notebook close, to take the longer route, and to let small observations become the beginning of something.
